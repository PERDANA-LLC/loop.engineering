**What's in it:**

- **View A / View B:** whether Jesus' groaning in John 11:33 and 11:38 is anger or grief. The STEPBible lexicon lists John 11:33 under "to be moved with anger", and the unit says so, while noting that grief can go with it.
- **Debate Corner:** what happens to believers between death and the resurrection, drawn from "sleepeth" (John 11:11) and John 11:25–26. It sets out three views:
  - the dead sleep, unconscious (Adventist and Jehovah's Witness teaching);
  - Bultmann's view that the resurrection is only a present reality of faith;
  - the believer lives with Christ now and is raised bodily at the last day.

  It argues for the third, with a Pastoral Warning about gravesides.
- **Council's Hard Questions:** why Jesus waited two days; why he groaned and wept, and how that shows both natures; and how Caiaphas could prophesy, with who "the children of God" in John 11:52 are.
- **Peter's restoration (decision D4):** John 11 uses both Greek words for love of Jesus' own love (John 11:3, 5, 36). The Deep Dive notes this only as a caution for John 21, without claiming it settles the question.
