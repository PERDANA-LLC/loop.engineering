- **The study itself:**
  - **Setting the Scene:** authorship is marked as tradition throughout, and John is shown beside Matthew, Mark and Luke as a fourth witness, not a rival.
  - **Chapter-by-chapter breakdown:** one paragraph per chapter, from John 1 to John 21.
  - **Tier 3 debate:** was John written to bring unbelievers to faith, to strengthen believers, or both? It argues for both, and mentions the manuscript difference in John 20:31.

After the checker passed on the unreferenced quotes, I also made a few content corrections of my own. I took out phrases quoted from the creeds. The feeding of the 5,000 is no longer called "the only sign in all four Gospels"; it is the only miracle besides the resurrection that all four record. I removed "sleeping disciples", which is from the other Gospels, not John. And Tier 1 now says the author is who Christians have long *believed* him to be.
